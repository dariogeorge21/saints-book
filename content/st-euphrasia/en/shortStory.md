# St. Euphrasia — Short Story

St. Euphrasia of the Sacred Heart of Jesus (1877–1952), affectionately remembered across Kerala as the "Praying Mother," was an Indian Carmelite nun renowned for her profound contemplative life, ceaseless adoration before the Blessed Sacrament, and unwavering devotion to the Rosary.

Born Rose Eluvathingal in Kattoor, Kerala, she joined the Congregation of the Mother of Carmel and spent almost five decades at St. Mary's Convent in Ollur. Her quiet ministry was marked by deep intercessory prayer, maternal counsel for those in grief, and steadfast spiritual surrender. She was canonised by Pope Francis on November 23, 2014, and her feast is observed on August 29.
