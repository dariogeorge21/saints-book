# St. Francis of Assisi — Short Story

St. Francis of Assisi (c. 1181–1226) is one of the most revered figures in Christian history. Founder of the Order of Friars Minor (Franciscans) and patron saint of ecology and animals, he renounced substantial family wealth to embrace a life of Gospel poverty, humility, and universal fraternity.

Celebrated for his Canticle of the Creatures, his peaceful encounter with Sultan al-Kamil, and his profound empathy with the suffering Christ, Francis received the stigmata in 1224. He was canonised in 1228 by Pope Gregory IX, and his feast day is celebrated on October 4.
